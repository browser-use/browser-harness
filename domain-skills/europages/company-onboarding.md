# Europages company onboarding

- Registration, email verification and the company listing are distinct states. Verified registration can lead to `/en/my-account/new-company` without creating a public company page.
- Inspect publish validation before assuming a country and email are sufficient. The company wizard can require phone number, street address, postal code, city, employee range, company type, delivery area and sector.
- Do not replace missing required facts with placeholders. Account creation is not a listing receipt.
