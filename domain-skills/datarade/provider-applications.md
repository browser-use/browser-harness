# Datarade provider applications

- `/apply` describes marketplace provider plans; `/apply/basics` starts the application.
- A commission-only plan has no annual subscription charge but includes a sales commission. Distinguish that from an entirely free commercial relationship.
- Application basics require contact details, registered legal company name, brand name and headquarters address. Data, Compliance and Documents follow.
- This is a provider onboarding/application flow, not a generic company directory. Do not claim ownership or commercialization rights over datasets without supporting facts.
