# BAI.tools submissions

- `/submit-ai-tools` begins with `#toolName`, `#toolUrl` and plan cards. The selected card has a highlighted border; inspect the free plan before submitting.
- The free plan can advertise a long review queue and no reciprocal-link requirement.
- Submit can lead to `/api/auth/signin` rather than a listing receipt. Authentication and submission are separate states.
- Google consent branding can differ from the directory brand. Inspect the displayed application name and the requested access before authorizing authentication; do not confuse a consent page with a successful submission.
