# EveryAI submissions

- `/submit` is a three-stage link import, detail review and confirmation flow. No account is advertised for the standard free listing.
- IDs include `f-url`, `f-name`, `f-desc`, `f-features`, `f-category`, `f-subcategory`, `f-email`, `f-phone` and `f-social`.
- The URL importer can combine the brand and website title in the name field. Inspect and correct imported copy before submission.
- Category selection populates the required subcategory; `business` offers options such as `sales` and `startup-tools`.
- The free radio is `input[name=plan][value=free]`. Free plans can prohibit editing after submission, so verify all details first.
- A filled, valid free form can still fail with Sign in to perform this action despite the no-account claim. Verify the actual result; do not count it as a submitted listing or use an admin login to bypass a public-route failure.
