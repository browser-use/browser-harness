# Viesearch free submissions

- Start at the site's add-URL route and inspect the current plan choices.
- Selecting the free plan can open a second upgrade panel. Its **Wait in Line** action completes the free queue choice.
- Email verification can return the browser to the plan chooser. Follow the free choice and **Wait in Line** again when that happens; clicking the email link alone does not prove entry into the review queue.
- Record the site reference and the final queue receipt. Check the resulting public page separately after moderation.
- Distinguish optional upgrades from required fees; do not count an account or verification email as a published backlink.
