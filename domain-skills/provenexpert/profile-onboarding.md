# ProvenExpert profile onboarding

- Email registration and verification precede the contact/profile wizard. A profile-title step does not establish a public company profile or website backlink.
- Industry is a searchable autocomplete. Type a matching service category, wait for the option, and commit the displayed result while the tab remains active.
- The company contact stage can require street, ZIP/postal code, and city. Country and business email alone may not be enough.
- Suggested Google business matches can be unrelated to the intended company. Never import a suggested entry merely because its name resembles the submitted brand.
- An automatically started premium trial without payment details is not evidence that permanent free-plan website links are included. Verify that entitlement separately.
